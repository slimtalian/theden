A library to make your own

Welcome to The Den.

A quiet home for your books, with a little of the feeling of holding one.

This sample collection explores the nineteenth century. These are short, original modern essays, with sources inside each book.

  1. Choose a spine. Drag the book to turn it in your hands.
  2. Open it, then choose scrolling or page-edge navigation.
  3. Download the app to build your own private, local library with calibre.
Get the app ↗

This demo has no account, uploads, analytics, or connection to anyone's personal library. Reading progress is stored only in this browser. GitHub serves the site.
